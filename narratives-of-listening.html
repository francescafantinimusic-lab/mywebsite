Narratives of Listening

Welcome! Thank you for staying curious from the start. On this page, I will share a series of listening meditations to explore in the days leading up to the workshop. Every moment is an invitation to listen. All you need is your attention, your willingness to go a little deeper than usual, and a notebook or a sheet of paper with a pencil, to note what you experience.

1. Pause. Listen to your breath. Notice one sound near you, one sound far away, and one sound inside memory.
